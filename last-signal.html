Someone is erasing Nora

You and your partner have just moved into a new apartment. In the closet lies a forgotten laptop. It belonged to the previous tenant, Nora Bennett, and it is still signed in to all her accounts.

Three weeks ago Nora went to film an abandoned psychiatric hospital for her channel, Nora After Dark, and never came back. The sheriff says she left on her own. Her mother still writes to her every day.

Read her messages, open her notes, listen to her voice memos and find out who is lying. Hurry: someone is remotely wiping the laptop, and her photos disappear while you watch.

Case filePrequel to Episode 1

Last Signal takes place a few days before Veil of Fear: He Never Left. What you find on Nora’s laptop leads straight to the place Jake will enter in Episode 1. Play it first, or play it later and see the story from the other side.

    One of you sits at Nora’s laptop: messages, photos, notes, mail, calls and her video channel.

  • Her notebook

  • Talk it through

    Three people lied about Nora. Argue, compare, and agree on who to believe.

  • Every minute counts

    Files vanish while you play. What you save at the end decides how her story ends.

Questions

Didn’t find your answer? Write to enigmastudio.md@gmail.com.

Do we need two devices?

It is best with a laptop or computer for one player and a phone for the other. If you only have one computer, the notebook can open in a second browser tab.

Can I play alone?

Yes. Open the notebook in a second tab and switch between them. But the game is made for two: it is more fun to argue about who lied.

Is it scary?

There are no jump scares and no gore. The fear comes from silence, sounds and the feeling that someone is watching. The story deals with a disappearance and death, so we recommend it for players 16 and older.

Do I need to know Veil of Fear?

No. Last Signal is a complete story on its own. Players of Episode 1 will recognise names and places.

How do I get the full game?

Press “Buy the full game” on this page: payment goes through itch.io. After paying you get a download page, and its link is also emailed to you. In the game press “I already bought it” and paste that link: the chapters open at once and your progress from the prologue stays.

Can we stop and continue later?

Yes. Progress saves after every solved puzzle. Open the game in the same browser and press Continue.
