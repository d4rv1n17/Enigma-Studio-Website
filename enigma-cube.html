A timer and a speedcubing school in one app

Enigma Cube is a timer and a speedcubing school in one app for Windows. It generates WCA scrambles, times you like a real Stackmat and calculates your averages and personal bests.

The Training section has courses for all 14 WCA events: from your very first solve to the methods the pros use. Every course has three levels: beginner, intermediate and advanced.

Everything works without the internet, and every solve stays on your computer.

Why Enigma Cube

One app instead of three

Usually a cuber keeps a timer in one tab, algorithm sheets in another and a trainer in a third. Learning apps are often made for phones, cover only the 3x3 or lock the advanced methods behind a payment. Enigma Cube puts all of it on your PC: a WCA timer, courses for every event and a trainer that tells you what to repeat. Free and offline.

Training

  • Courses for every event

Installing and updating

Enigma Cube is not yet signed with a paid code certificate, so Windows may show a blue “Windows protected your PC” window the first time. This is normal for small independent apps. If an older version is installed, the installer updates it and keeps all your solves.

  1. Run EnigmaCube-Setup-3.1.exe.
  2. If the blue window appears, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Choose the folder and shortcuts and click Install (or Update).
