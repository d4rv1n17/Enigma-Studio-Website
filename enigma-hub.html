One hub. More to discover.

Our apps and games used to live on different pages, each with its own installer and its own way of updating. Enigma Hub brings them together, and from now on our apps are installed through it.

Install Enigma Cube or Enigma Disk in one click, launch them from one window and get updates without searching for them. Our games open from the same place, and studio news arrives right inside the app.

Hub installs only for your Windows account and does not need administrator rights. Your settings and apps stay where they are.

  • Every app in one place

    Install, launch, update and remove Enigma Cube and Enigma Disk without visiting any download page.

  • Verified downloads

    Installers come only from our official GitHub releases and are checked by their SHA-256. Unfinished downloads are deleted.

  • Our games

    Launch the studio games you already have, including Steam libraries on any drive, or open their store pages. Hub never takes payments.

  • Your library

    Search, favourites, sorting by last launch and quick launch of the three apps you used most recently.

  • Studio news

    Release notes for every version right inside Hub, saved for reading offline.

  • Smart updates

    Hub updates itself in the background. Apps are updated only when a newer version is really out.

  • Download panel

    Speed, time left and a cancel button. Free space is checked before anything is downloaded.

  • Six languages

    English, Russian, Romanian, German, Spanish and French. The language changes instantly.

  • Keeps what you have

    Already installed Cube or Disk? Connect it without reinstalling. A local history shows every launch, install and update.

How it works

Three steps, one window

  1. 1

    Install Hub

    Run the installer, choose a folder and whether you want a desktop shortcut. No administrator rights needed.

  2. 2

    Pick an app

    Open Discover and press Install. Hub downloads the official release, checks its SHA-256 and installs it for you.

  3. 3

    Launch and stay up to date

    Start apps and games from Home or My library. When a new version comes out, the Update button appears by itself.

The installer

  • Installs for the current user, no administrator rights needed.
  • Lets you choose the folder and a desktop shortcut.
  • Shows up in Settings → Apps, where it can be uninstalled.
  • Removing Hub keeps your installed apps and your settings.
  • New Hub versions download in the background and install after a restart.

Installing

Enigma Hub is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may show a blue “Windows protected your PC” window the first time. This is normal for small independent apps.

  1. Download and run EnigmaHubSetup.exe.
  2. If the blue window appears, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Choose a folder, finish the installer and open Discover in Hub.
