What we do for you

You make the game, we take care of the release

  • Release on Google Play

    We publish the game from our developer account: no fee, no verification and no tester group needed on your side.

  • Store page

    Title, description, icon, screenshots and age rating. We can write the texts in English, Russian, Romanian and Ukrainian.

  • Build and testing

    We help prepare the Android build, check it on real phones and send you what needs fixing before release.

  • Updates

    You send a new version, we publish it. Fixes and new content reach your players without the store paperwork.

  • Promotion

    A news post on our site, posts on our social pages and help with the trailer and screenshots.

  • Player feedback

    We read the reviews and crash reports and pass you everything that matters.

How it works

Four steps from your build to the store

  1. 1

    Send your game

    A link to the build or the project, a few screenshots and a short description: what the game is and who it is for.

  2. 2

    We play it

    We play the game, check it against the Google Play rules and reply with what we think.

  3. 3

    We agree

    We discuss the terms for your game and prepare the store page together.

  4. 4

    Release

    We publish the game and keep it updated with every new version you send.

What we need from you

  • A game for Android, or a Unity or Godot project we can build for Android
  • All code, art, music and sounds are yours or properly licensed
  • The content follows the Google Play rules
  • A working build, a few screenshots and a short description
Why not publish it myself?

You can, but Google Play asks for a paid developer account, identity verification and, for new personal accounts, a closed test with a group of testers before release. We already have all of that.

How much does it cost?

We agree on the terms for each game separately. Write to us about your game and we will answer.

Only Google Play?

For now, yes. We want to add more stores later.

Does it have to be a horror game?

No. We make horror ourselves, but we are happy to publish games of any genre.

My game is not finished yet. Can I still write?

Yes. Send what you have: we can tell you early what to prepare for the store.
