Three days to get out

The twins have locked you inside the school. Now you have to get out, and you have to be careful and fast, because the twins are looking for you everywhere.

Search the classrooms, the halls and the basement for keys. Fight off the monsters that stand in your way, keep your flashlight alive and stay out of the twins’ sight.

You have three days. Good luck.

  • Keep out of the twins

    They hunt you through the whole school. Hide, run, and never let them see you twice.

  • Explore the school

    Classrooms, corridors and a basement full of danger. Every room may hide a key.

  • Collect batteries

    Your flashlight is your best friend. Without batteries, the dark wins.

  • Find the way out

    Collect items and keys, fight the monsters and escape before the third day ends.
