Enigma Studio is an independent team driven by a passion for mystery, horror, and psychological storytelling. We craft deeply immersive experiences that challenge perception and provoke thought.

Our mission is to create games that stay with players long after they’ve put down the controller. From eerie psychological thrillers to haunting post-apocalyptic worlds, we push the boundaries of storytelling and gameplay.

Games are the heart of the studio, but not the only thing we do: alongside our own titles, the team also takes on other creative projects.

  • Main focus

    Games

    Story-driven horror and psychological games for PC, built around atmosphere, sound and the fear of what hides in the dark.

  • Also

    Other projects

    Beyond our own games, we work on other creative projects, bringing the same attention to mood and detail.

Join us as we explore the unknown.
