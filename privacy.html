Privacy Policy

Short and honest: what data our website touches, why, and what you can do about it.

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Who we are

This website belongs to Enigma Studio, an independent game development team. If you have any question about your data, write to us at enigmastudio.md@gmail.com.

We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use any tracking or analytics on this website.

Newsletter

If you subscribe to our news, we collect only your email address. We use it to send you news about our games and apps: releases, updates and announcements. Nothing else.

The newsletter is run by MailerLite, an email service that stores the list of subscribers for us. After you subscribe, MailerLite sends you an email to confirm the subscription. Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to remove your address at any time.

We keep your address while you are subscribed. When you unsubscribe, we stop sending you emails.

Contacting us

The contact form on our website does not send or store anything. It only prepares a letter in your own email app. When you send it, we receive your email address, your name if you wrote it, and your message, and we use them only to answer you.

Third-party services

Some parts of the website load content from other companies. When that happens, your browser connects to their servers, and they can see your IP address and basic technical information, as with any website:

  • Google Fonts — the fonts of the website.
  • YouTube — video previews and players. The player only loads when you press play, in YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode.
  • rss2json and noembed — help us find the newest video of our YouTube playlist.
  • MailerLite — receives your email address when you subscribe.
  • GitHub — downloads of Enigma Disk and the source code of our apps.
  • Dalink — the donation page you open with “Support us”. It is a separate website with its own rules.
  • Google Play, Steam, App Store and social networks — only when you follow a link to them.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Cookies and local storage

We do not set cookies. Your browser remembers only your chosen language and a small note for smooth page transitions. This stays on your device and you can clear it in your browser settings at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we have about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and withdraw your consent to the newsletter at any time. Just write to enigmastudio.md@gmail.com and we will answer as soon as we can.

Changes

If anything here changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
