Questions we get

Short answers to what people ask us most. Didn’t find yours? Just write.

When does Veil of Fear come out?

There is no release date yet. The first episode, He Never Left, is in development. Follow us on YouTube to see the progress, and the Steam page will appear on the game’s page as soon as it is live.

Will Twins’ Hunt come to iPhone?

Yes. The App Store release is coming soon, together with a big update for all players. On Android the game is already available on Google Play.

Is Enigma Cube free?

Yes, completely free for Windows 10 and 11. You can download it on the Enigma Cube page.

I found a bug. Where do I report it?

Pick “Players & feedback” above and tell us what happened, on which device, and add a screenshot if you can. Every report helps.

Can I play your games on stream or YouTube?

We love seeing that. Pick “Streamers & creators” above and tell us about your channel, so we can watch and share it.
